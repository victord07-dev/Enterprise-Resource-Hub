🧾 2. FINAL LEDGER SCHEMA (USE THIS)
InventoryLedger {
  id

  productId
  warehouseId

  type: "GRN" | "DISPATCH" | "ADJUSTMENT"

  quantityChange   // +ve or -ve

  referenceType: "GRN" | "CHALLAN" | "MANUAL"
  referenceId

  createdAt
}
🔑 IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES
❌ Never update stock directly
✅ Always go through ledger
❌ Never edit/delete ledger entries
✅ Always append new entries

🔧 What it MUST do:
Insert ledger entry
Update inventory snapshot
Validate stock (no negative)

GRN Confirm

Dispatch (Challan)
