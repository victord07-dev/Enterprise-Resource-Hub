🧠 What & Why

Products are procured in batches (lots) with varying landed costs over time.

Your system:

Uses WAC (Weighted Average Cost) → accounting ✔
Needs FIFO (Lot Engine) → pricing & margin ✔
🔒 Cost Strategy (MANDATORY — DO NOT CHANGE)
WAC (product.costPrice):
- Used for accounting, financial reporting
- MUST NOT be modified

FIFO (Lot Engine):
- Used for pricing, margin control, discount decisions
- Runs in parallel to WAC
- MUST NOT replace WAC

1. Load Data

3. Landed Cost Formula (FINAL)

GRN.deliveryCost × (lineItemQty / totalGRNQty)
landedCost =
(buyingPrice × qty + allocatedDeliveryCost) ÷ qty

📊 Floor Calculations (FINAL — NO DEVIATION)
lotFloor    = landedCost × (1 + minMarginPct / 100)

blendedCost = weighted avg of remaining lots

globalFloor = blendedCost × (1 + minMarginPct / 100)

strictFloor = MAX(lotFloor)
