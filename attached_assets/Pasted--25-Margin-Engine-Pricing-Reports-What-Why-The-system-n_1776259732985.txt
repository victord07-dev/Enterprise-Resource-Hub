#25 — Margin Engine & Pricing Reports
🧠 What & Why

The system now supports:

FIFO lot-based cost (#22)
Daily pricing with approval workflow (#27)
Full pricing UI and sales integration (#28)

However, there is no management-level visibility into:

overall profitability
pricing risk
products requiring action
💡 Goal:
Provide a real-time pricing and margin dashboard
that converts cost + pricing data into business decisions.
✅ Done Looks Like
1. Reports → Daily Pricing Dashboard

A new page under:

Reports → Daily Pricing
2. Portfolio Summary (Top Cards)

Display 4 summary cards:

🟢 Total Inventory Cost
Sum of (remaining stock × blendedCost)
🟢 Expected Revenue
Sum of (remaining stock × effectivePrice)
🟡 Required Revenue (Min Margin)
Sum of (remaining stock × globalFloor)
🔴 Portfolio Status
If Expected Revenue ≥ Required Revenue → SAFE
Else → AT RISK
3. Product-Level Table
Columns:
Product | Stock Qty | Avg Cost | Effective Price | Global Floor | Margin % | Source | Risk | Needs Pricing
Calculations:
Margin %
((effectivePrice - blendedCost) / blendedCost) × 100
Risk Indicator
costRatio = blendedCost / effectivePrice

0.9 → 🔴 High Risk

0.75 → 🟡 Medium

else → 🟢 Safe
Source

From:

/api/daily-price-sheets/effective-prices-today

Values:

"today" | "fallback" | "none"
⚠️ Special Handling — No Confirmed Price

If:

source = "none"

Then:

show badge: "Needs Pricing"
exclude from Expected Revenue calculation ❗
include in a separate count
4. Actionable Insights Section
🟡 Needs Pricing
products.needsPricingReview = true OR source = "none"
🔴 High Risk Products
marginPct < product.minMarginPct
🔥 Old Stock Pressure
stock age > 30 days AND qty > minStockLevel
5. Sales Order — Lot-Level Margin
📍 In Sales Order Detail Page

For each dispatched line item:

Cost (FIFO): ₹40,000  
Selling Price: ₹45,000  
Margin: 12.5%
If multiple lots used:
Use weighted average cost based on FIFO consumption
🔌 API — Pricing Summary
Endpoint:
GET /api/reports/pricing-summary
Response Structure:
Per Product:
{
  productId,
  productName,
  stockQty,
  blendedCost,
  effectivePrice,
  globalFloor,
  marginPct,
  riskLevel,
  source,
  needsPricingReview
}
Portfolio:
{
  totalCost,
  expectedRevenue,
  requiredRevenue,
  status: "SAFE" | "AT_RISK",
  productsWithoutPriceCount
}
⚠️ Business Rules (MANDATORY)
1. Use Effective Price ONLY
Do NOT use product.unitPrice for reporting
2. Exclude "No Price" Products from Revenue
source = "none" → exclude from expectedRevenue
3. FIFO Cost Only
Use blendedCost from lot engine (not WAC directly)
4. Warehouse Support (Optional Filter)

Allow filtering by:

warehouseId (if provided)
