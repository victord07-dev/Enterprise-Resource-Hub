REQUIRED tables to propose:

1. fixed_assets
   Must support: asset name, category (machinery/vehicle/
   equipment/furniture/other), purchase date, purchase 
   value, useful life in years, depreciation method 
   (SLM = Straight Line Method is Indian statutory 
   default), accumulated depreciation, current book 
   value, notes, is_active flag

2. loans
   Must support: lender name, loan type (short_term = 
   ≤1 year / long_term = >1 year), sanctioned amount, 
   outstanding amount, interest rate %, disbursement 
   date, maturity date, repayment schedule notes, 
   linked cash account (where loan proceeds were 
   received), notes

3. equity_accounts
   Must support: account name, account type 
   (share_capital / retained_earnings / owners_capital / 
   other), opening balance, opening balance date, notes

4. opening_balances
   This is the go-live opening balance table. Must 
   support entering starting values for:
   - Accounts Receivable (total AR as at go-live date 
     — separate from ongoing invoices the ERP tracks)
   - Accounts Payable (total AP as at go-live date)
   - Inventory value (total stock value as at go-live)
   - Any other current asset/liability category
   Must support: account_type enum, amount, 
   as_of_date, notes
