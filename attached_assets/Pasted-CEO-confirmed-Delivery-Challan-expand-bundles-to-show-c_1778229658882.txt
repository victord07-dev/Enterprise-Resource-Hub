STOP CHECKPOINT — Invoice pricing decision needed
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before implementing Fix 5 (sales invoice expansion), 
post your answer to this question:

When a bundle is sold at ₹40,000 (e.g., Solar Kit 2KVA) 
and the invoice must list individual components, what 
price should each component carry on the invoice?

Option P1 — Proportional distribution (recommended):
Each component's invoice value = 
  (component_standard_price ÷ sum_of_all_component_prices) 
  × bundle_sold_price × qty_in_bundle
This ensures invoice total = SO total exactly. Each 
component gets its own HSN + GST rate applied to its 
proportional share.

Option P2 — Component own price:
Each component's invoice value = its own product 
selling price × qty. Invoice total may differ from 
SO total if the bundle had a custom price. GST is 
applied to each component's standard price.

Post your recommendation and await operator approval 
before writing Fix 5.
