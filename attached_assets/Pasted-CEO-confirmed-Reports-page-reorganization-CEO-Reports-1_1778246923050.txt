──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CEO REPORT 1 — Period-wise Sales
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Aggregator: getPeriodSales(from, to, granularity?)
- Auto-bucket if granularity not passed
- SUM(sales_invoices.grand_total) per bucket, 
  exclude cancelled
- Return: [{period_label, period_start, total_sales, 
  invoice_count}], grand_total, avg_per_bucket, 
  peak_bucket, peak_amount

Route: GET /api/reports/period-sales?from=&to=&granularity=

Component: PeriodSales
- DateRangeFilter + granularity override toggle
- Bar chart (Recharts): x=period, y=₹ sales
- Table: Period | Total Sales | Invoice Count
- Summary cards: Total, Average, Peak
- 3 download buttons (CSV, Excel, PDF)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CEO REPORT 2 — Period-wise Profit
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Aggregator: getPeriodProfit(from, to, granularity?)
- Per bucket: revenue (sales invoices), purchases 
  (supplier invoices as proxy COGS), expenses
- profit = revenue - purchases - expenses
- margin_pct = profit / revenue × 100

Route: GET /api/reports/period-profit?from=&to=&granularity=

Component: PeriodProfit
- DateRangeFilter + granularity toggle
- Grouped bar chart: Revenue / Purchases / Profit
- Table: Period | Revenue | Purchases | Expenses | 
  Profit | Margin %
- Profit cells green if positive, red if negative
- 3 download buttons

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CEO REPORT 3 — Product-wise Sales
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Aggregator: getProductSales(from, to, productId?)
- JOIN sales_invoice_items → sales_invoices → products
- Exclude type='bundle'
- Group by product: SUM(qty), SUM(totalAmount), 
  COUNT(distinct invoices)
- Sort by total_sales DESC

Route: GET /api/reports/product-sales?from=&to=&productId=

Component: ProductSales
- DateRangeFilter + optional product search filter
- Horizontal bar chart: top 10 products by sales
- Table: Product | SKU | Category | Qty Sold | 
  Total Sales | Invoice Count
- 3 download buttons

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CEO REPORT 4 — Product-wise Profit (FIFO)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Aggregator: getProductWiseProfit(from, to)
- Dispatched challans in period, exclude bundles
- FIFO cost via computeFifoLots per product
- Gross profit = revenue - FIFO cost
- Zero-cost lines: show with "No GRN cost data" flag

Route: GET /api/reports/product-profit?from=&to=

Component: ProductProfit
- DateRangeFilter
- Bar chart: top 10 by gross profit
- Table: Product | SKU | Qty | Revenue | Cost | 
  Gross Profit | Margin % | GRN flag
- Footer: "Bundle products excluded — component-level 
  costing deferred to Phase 4D" always visible
- 3 download buttons
