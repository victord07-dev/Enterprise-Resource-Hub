Good — the Replit AI's diagnosis is mostly correct, but let me give you a clearer picture and flag a couple of things it glossed over.
On the two issues
Issue 1: New messages not flowing into your inbox (webhook problem)
The diagnosis is right. The sequence is: Interakt tried delivering webhooks → got 404s (because routes weren't deployed yet) → after N consecutive failures, Interakt's webhook system either backs off with exponential retry delays or disables the endpoint until you re-verify it. This is standard behaviour for any webhook provider.
The fix suggested — re-save the webhook URL or hit "Test webhook" — is correct and should work. A few additional things worth checking that the Replit AI didn't mention:

Verify the webhook URL is exactly right in Interakt — including https://, correct domain, and the full path (/api/whatsapp/webhook). A trailing slash mismatch can cause silent 404s.
Check that your production server actually responds with 200 (not 202, not 301 redirect) to the test. Some webhook systems treat redirects as failures.
Verify webhook signature/secret validation isn't rejecting Interakt's requests. If you added HMAC verification and the secret is wrong, your endpoint will reject with 401/403 and Interakt will keep retrying then back off.
Check the webhook event subscriptions in Interakt — you need to have subscribed to the right events (message received, message sent, message status, etc.). If only some are subscribed, you'll get partial delivery.
Look at Interakt's webhook delivery logs — Interakt's dashboard has a "Webhook logs" or "Delivery attempts" section. This tells you exactly what they're sending and what response they're getting. Don't rely only on your server logs.

Issue 2: Old chats not showing up (backfill problem)
The diagnosis is correct — webhooks are forward-only by design. They only notify you of events from the moment of subscription. Your existing conversation history in Interakt's dashboard lives in their database, not yours, so until you pull it in via their REST API, it won't appear in your inbox.
This is not a bug; it's just that the scope was webhook-only. But for a production inbox replacing Interakt's dashboard, backfill is not optional — your team needs conversation continuity.
My answer to A vs B: do both, in this order

First, fix the webhook (A) — confirm it's live and receiving. Without this, backfill is pointless because any messages arriving during the backfill window will be missed.
Then plan the backfill (B) — but do it properly, not as a one-shot script.

What a proper backfill should include (push back on Replit AI here)
A "one-time backfill script" sounds simple but has real gotchas at your scale. Make sure whoever builds it handles:

Pagination — Interakt's /conversations and /messages endpoints are paginated; you'll need to loop through all pages, not just the first.
Rate limiting — Interakt has API rate limits (typically 60–100 requests/minute for their public API). A naive script will hit 429s and skip data. Build in exponential backoff and resume-from-checkpoint.
Idempotency — each Interakt message has a unique ID. Upsert on that ID so re-running the backfill doesn't duplicate messages.
The overlap window — between when webhooks went live and when backfill runs, some messages arrive via both paths. Deduplication by message ID handles this cleanly.
Media attachments — images, PDFs, voice notes referenced in old conversations are stored on Interakt's CDN. Decide whether to proxy the URLs (fragile if Interakt expires them) or download and re-host them yourself. For a distribution business sending invoice PDFs and product images via WhatsApp, you almost certainly want to re-host.
Contact/customer linking — messages in Interakt are tied to phone numbers; in your ERP they should be tied to customer records. Build the phone-number-to-customer mapping during backfill, and handle the "unknown number" case.
Conversation threading logic — Interakt's concept of a "conversation" (24-hour session window, per WhatsApp's rules) may not match what you want to display. Decide whether to preserve their session breaks or show one continuous thread per customer.
Status history — sent / delivered / read / failed statuses per message. If you only import message content and not status, your inbox will look incomplete.

One more thing worth flagging
Beyond backfill, think about ongoing sync resilience. Webhooks can still miss events — network blips, your server going down for a deploy, Interakt outages. A production-grade WhatsApp inbox usually runs a reconciliation job (say, every 6 or 24 hours) that pulls recent conversations from Interakt's REST API and fills in anything the webhook missed. Worth adding to the scope now rather than discovering gaps three months in.
So my recommendation: tell Replit AI "do A now, then scope B properly with the points above, and also add a daily reconciliation job as part of B." That's what turns this from a prototype integration into something you can actually run a business on.
