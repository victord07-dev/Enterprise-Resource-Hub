PRE-FIX AUDIT — CREDIT OVERRIDE FOR SALES & PURCHASE PAYMENT GATES

CEO has confirmed that:
1. Sometimes ITFI dispatches sales orders without 100% customer payment
   (credit sales to trusted customers)
2. Sometimes ITFI receives goods (GRN) without 100% supplier payment
   (taking goods on credit from trusted suppliers)

The current Phase 1 hard-block gates need to change to allow controlled
override-with-reason. Before I design the fix plan, I need a thorough
audit of every place these gates exist and every related code path.

This is investigation only. DO NOT change any code. DO NOT propose
solutions. Report findings only.
