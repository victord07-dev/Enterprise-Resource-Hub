Phase 4A: Credit Override + Supplier Invoice Auto-Create + DueDate Fix
         + PO GST + Outstanding-Dues Block

CONTEXT

CEO has confirmed multiple operational realities not currently
reflected in the system:

1. Sales orders sometimes dispatch without 100% customer payment
   (credit sales). Phase 1 hard-block contradicts business reality.

2. GRNs sometimes received without 100% supplier payment (credit
   purchases). Phase 1 hard-block contradicts reality.

3. AP aging is broken because supplier invoices aren't created (0
   rows for 7 confirmed GRNs).

4. AR aging is broken because sales_invoice.dueDate is always null
   on auto-creation.

5. Purchase orders capture no GST per line — totals are tax-exclusive,
   PO PDF reads GST live from products at render time (snapshot risk).

6. Customers with outstanding dues can have new sales orders created
   for them, leading to growing unpaid balances.

This phase implements coordinated fixes for all six gaps:
- Sales-side credit override (admin-only, mirror of pricing-floor-
  override pattern)
- Purchase-side credit override (admin + accountant)
- Auto-create supplier_invoice on GRN confirm (upload-shell pattern,
  mirror of Phase 1 sales_invoice auto-create)
- Sales invoice dueDate computed from customer payment_terms
- PO line items get GST snapshot (gst_rate + hsn_code + gst_amount)
  at PR-to-PO conversion or PO line entry
- New SO creation blocked if customer has outstanding dues (admin
  override allowed)
- Customer/supplier payment_terms field
- Audit log additions for payment recording
- AR/AP aging credit visibility

After this ships, ITFI's credit handling, AR aging, AP aging, and
PO GST tracking match real operational reality.

     may now require more payment than before to clear the gate.

     Migration handling:
     For 21 currently-underpaid POs, compute the payment shortfall

       new_outstanding = grand_total - sum(supplier_payments)
     Report a migration table to operator showing each PO's old

        title=`Credit dispatch approved: Challan ${challan.number}`,
        message=`Admin approved ₹${creditAmount} credit on Challan
        ${challan.number}. Reason: ${creditReason}`, relatedId=challan.id)
