Phase 4C: Financial Dashboard + Reports

CONTEXT

Phase 4A and 4B closed cleanly. Phase 4 Cleanup closed. ITFI ERP 
now has full transactional integrity (credit overrides, supplier 
invoices, dues blocks, PO GST, cash accounts, transfers, 
adjustments) but the financial visibility layer is fake.

The current home dashboard shows hardcoded widgets. There are no 
downloadable reports for CEO month-end review or accountant Tally 
reconciliation. Everything happens at row-level — no aggregate 
visibility.

This phase ships:
- Live dashboard with real cross-account financial widgets
- 11 downloadable financial reports (PDF + Excel)
- Common date-range filtering infrastructure
- Pending Actions widget for CEO operational awareness

After this phase, ITFI is genuinely production-ready. CEO can run 
the business from the dashboard. Accountant can export reports for 
Tally entry. Audit trail supports both views.

What this phase does NOT deliver (deferred):
- Period comparisons (vs Last Month, vs Last Year) — not enough 
  historical data yet
- Live forecasting / cash flow projections
- Budget vs actual reports
- Bank statement reconciliation (separate future phase)
- Email scheduled reports
- GSTR filing automation (Tally handles)
- Multi-currency

SECTION C — REPORT 1: P&L STATEMENT
==========================================================

C1. Page: /reports → Financial Reports tab → P&L Statement subtab
    OR /reports/pl-statement (whichever pattern exists)
    
    Authorization: admin + accountant only

C2. Filters:
    - Date Range Filter component (Section A4)
    - Default: This Month

C3. P&L structure:
    
    REVENUE
      Sales Revenue (from sales_invoices.grand_total in period)
        - Less: Discounts (if tracked)
        - Less: Returns (if tracked)
      Net Revenue: ₹X
    
    COST OF GOODS SOLD
      Material costs (from products purchased + dispatched in period)
        - For each dispatched item: quantity × FIFO buying_price
      Direct logistics (purchase delivery_cost portion)
      Total COGS: ₹Y
    
    GROSS PROFIT: ₹(X - Y)
    Gross Margin: ((X-Y)/X) × 100 = Z%
    
    OPERATING EXPENSES (categorized from expenses table)
      Rent: ₹A
      Salaries: ₹B
      Utilities: ₹C
      Transportation: ₹D
      Marketing: ₹E
      Office Supplies: ₹F
      Other: ₹G
      Total Operating Expenses: ₹H
    
    NET PROFIT: ₹(X - Y - H)
    Net Margin: ((X-Y-H)/X) × 100 = K%

C4. Display:
    On-page table with collapsible sections
    Numbers right-aligned, Indian comma format (12,34,567.89)
    Negative values in red
    Bold for major totals (Net Revenue, Gross Profit, Net Profit)

C5. Export buttons:
    - "Download PDF" button → generates PDF via Section A5
    - "Download Excel" button → generates Excel via Section A6
    - Each download logs to report_generation_log

C6. PDF format:
    ITFI letterhead (top)
    "Profit & Loss Statement"
    "Period: [from] to [to]"
    "Generated: [timestamp] by [user]"
    Full P&L table
    Page numbers
    "Generated from ITFI ERP" footer

C7. Excel format:
    Sheet 1: P&L summary (same as PDF)
    Sheet 2: Detailed line items (every sales invoice + every 
             expense in period for accountant verification)
