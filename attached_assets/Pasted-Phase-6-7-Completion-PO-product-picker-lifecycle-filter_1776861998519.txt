Phase 6.7 Completion — PO product picker lifecycle filtering
Check 6 of the Phase 6.7 stop gate revealed a gap: the Supply Chain PO product picker has no lifecycle filtering. Products with lifecycleStatus IN (draft, discontinued, replaced) appear as selectable with no warning, letting procurement accidentally raise fresh POs for retired SKUs. Sales-side already blocks this per Phase 6.5 Section F. Procurement-side needs the same boundary for consistency.
Scope (narrow — do not expand):
Fix 1 — PO product picker in Supply Chain
Locate the product picker in the PO line-items editor in SupplyChain.tsx (wherever product selection happens when adding a line item to a PO). Apply the exact same pattern as the Sales picker per Phase 7.5 Section B:

Draft / discontinued / replaced products remain visible in the dropdown
Product name is followed by suffix text in red: (Not selectable — draft) / (Not selectable — discontinued) / (Not selectable — replaced)
Selection hard-blocked with a toast
For replaced products with replacedByProductId set: toast offers "Switch to replacement" action button. Clicking it selects the replacement product with price/GST/HSN auto-filled — same behavior as sales side

Fix 2 — Consistent behavior in auto-PR generation
When checkAndCreatePurchaseRequests runs (Phase 6.6 Section C5 codepath) and evaluates bundle component shortages, it should NOT include line items whose component's lifecycleStatus IN (draft, discontinued, replaced). Instead, emit a warning in the PR notes: "Component [name] is [status] — procurement of this SKU was skipped. Consider the replacement." This prevents auto-generated PRs from accidentally ordering retired SKUs.
Fix 3 — Historical integrity preserved
Pre-existing PO-2026-0017 (with LIB-160 pending) is NOT affected. Opening an existing PO continues to show its historical line items unchanged. Only NEW POs (or edits to new lines on existing POs) hit the picker block.
