REPORT 2 — Period Profit
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Aggregator: getPeriodProfit(from, to, granularity?)
- Same bucketing as Report 1
- Per bucket:
    revenue = SUM(sales_invoices.grand_total) excl. cancelled
    purchases = SUM(supplier_invoices.total_amount) excl. 
      cancelled (proxy COGS, matched by date bucket)
    expenses = SUM(expenses.amount)
    profit = revenue - purchases - expenses
    margin_pct = profit / revenue × 100 (null if revenue=0)
- Return: { buckets: [{period_label, revenue, purchases, 
  expenses, profit, margin_pct}], summary: {total_revenue, 
  total_profit, avg_margin_pct} }

Route: GET /api/reports/period-profit?from=&to=&granularity=

Component: PeriodProfit.tsx
- DateRangeFilter + granularity toggle
- Grouped bar chart: Revenue (blue) / Purchases (orange) / 
  Profit (green or red depending on sign)
- Summary cards: Total Revenue | Total Profit | Avg Margin %
- Table: Period | Revenue | Purchases | Expenses | 
  Profit | Margin %
- Profit cell: green text if positive, red if negative
- 3 download buttons

REPORT 4 — Product Profit (FIFO dispatch-time)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Aggregator: getProductWiseProfit(from, to)
Design constraints locked — implement exactly:

1. Fetch delivery_challans WHERE status='dispatched' 
   AND dispatch_date BETWEEN from AND to
2. For each challan item where product.type = 'product' 
   or 'physical' (exclude 'bundle' and 'service'):
   - FIFO cost at dispatch date via computeFifoLots
   - revenue = qty_dispatched × SO item unitPrice
   - cost = FIFO blended cost × qty_dispatched
   - gross_profit = revenue - cost
   - has_grn_data = cost > 0
3. Group by productId, aggregate across period
4. Return: [{product_name, sku, qty_sold, revenue, 
  cost, gross_profit, margin_pct, has_grn_data}]

Route: GET /api/reports/product-profit?from=&to=

Component: ProductProfit.tsx
- DateRangeFilter only (no granularity — product-level)
- Bar chart: top 10 products by gross_profit
- Table: Product | SKU | Qty | Revenue | Cost | 
  Gross Profit | Margin % | GRN Status
  (flag icon + "No GRN cost data" on rows where 
   has_grn_data = false — never show 100% margin silently)
- Footer note always visible: "Bundle products excluded 
  — component-level costing deferred to Phase 4D"
- 3 download buttons
