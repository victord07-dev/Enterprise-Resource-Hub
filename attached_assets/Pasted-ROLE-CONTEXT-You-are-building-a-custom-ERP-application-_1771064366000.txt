ROLE & CONTEXT

You are building a custom ERP application for a distributor dealing in solar panels, electronics, and commodities.

The goal is to automate day-to-day business operations so that:

Employees work entirely inside the system

The owner only monitors dashboards and receives payment alerts

All actual payments happen outside the application

This is a business-critical system, not a demo.

CORE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

DO NOT implement payment execution

DO NOT integrate bank APIs, OTP approvals, or payment gateways

Payment requests are tracking + notification only

Owner receives WhatsApp notification when payment is due

Owner decides and pays manually outside the system

The system must maintain a complete audit trail

If a feature conflicts with these rules, do not build it.

USER ROLES

Owner (read-only dashboards, notifications)

Manager

Sales

Accounts

Operations

Field Staff

Each role must have strict access boundaries.

MODULES TO BUILD
1️⃣ SALES & PROJECT MANAGEMENT

What it should do

Capture leads

Generate quotations

Show profit/margin before confirmation

Apply role-based discount limits

Convert quotation into sale or project

Track project milestones and delivery/installation

Workflow

Lead → Quotation → Approval → Sale / Project → Execution

2️⃣ INVENTORY & STOCK MANAGEMENT

What it should do

Maintain product master (solar, electronics, commodities)

Track stock by warehouse/location

Support batch/lot tracking for commodities

Support serial tracking for electronics

Reserve stock on confirmed sale

Track stock inward and outward

Alert on low stock

3️⃣ SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

What it should do

Maintain vendor database

Create and track purchase orders

Record material inward

Track logistics and transport details

Record supplier invoices

Show vendor aging and outstanding amounts

4️⃣ PAYMENT TRACKING & NOTIFICATION (CRITICAL)

Very Important: This module is NOT for payments

What it should do

Allow employees to create a payment request

Capture:

Amount

Vendor / party

Product or project reference

Due date

Remarks

Optional attachment (bill / invoice image)

Automatically send a WhatsApp notification to the owner

Track payment status:

Pending

Partially Paid

Paid

Allow upload of payment proof after manual payment

Workflow

Payment Request Created
        ↓
WhatsApp Alert Sent to Owner
        ↓
Owner Pays Manually (Outside System)
        ↓
Status Updated for Records


Never add approval buttons or payment triggers.

5️⃣ AUTOMATED REMINDERS

What it should do

Send up to 3 reminders per day

Support reminders for:

Customer receivables

Supplier payables

Allow configurable reminder timings

Notify via WhatsApp, SMS, or in-app notification

6️⃣ COMMISSION & LABOUR COST MANAGEMENT

Commission

Fixed or percentage based

Calculated per sale or project

Agent-wise commission ledger

Labour Cost

Per project

Per sale

Daily wages

Monthly salary

Auto-linked to attendance and project assignment

7️⃣ EMPLOYEE MANAGEMENT (HRMS)

Attendance

Late entry penalty rules

Lunch break fixed at 45 minutes

Overtime calculation

Leave & Salary

Leave application and approval

Salary auto-calculation

Deductions and incentives

Payslip generation

8️⃣ LIVE LOCATION & SELFIE TRACKING

What it should do

Track live GPS location of field staff

Store route history

Support geo-fencing (office/site)

Require selfies at:

Login

Site arrival

Logout

Ensure selfie is captured via camera only

Store time and location with every selfie

9️⃣ DASHBOARDS & REPORTS
