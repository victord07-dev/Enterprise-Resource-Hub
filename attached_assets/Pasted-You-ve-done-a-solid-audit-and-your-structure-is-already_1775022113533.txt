✅ 2. Add File Attachments (Generic, Reusable Design)

DO NOT attach files directly in GRN or invoice tables.

👉 Create a generic attachment system

New Table: attachments
{
  _id: ObjectId,

  entityType: "GRN" | "SupplierInvoice" | "SalesInvoice" | "SalesReturn",

  entityId: ObjectId,

  fileUrl: string,
  fileName: string,
  fileType: string,

  uploadedAt: Date
}
🧠 Why this design is correct:

✔ Scales for future modules (audit, projects, etc.)
